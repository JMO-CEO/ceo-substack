They think they formed a joint venture. Their bank feed proves they formed a mess.

### The dark bank feed trap

Say you got your exemption right and your LLC is properly filed. Most spec JVs still fail the basic transparency test on day one.

I ask operators the same five questions during diligence.

Where is the partnership bank account? Show me the signed JV Terms. Show me who approved the loan draw. Show me the related party fee vote. Show me the audit log.

Silence. Then a shared folder with three PDFs named Final_FINAL_v2.

No connected bank account. No approval workflow before debt or overruns. No timestamped log of who moved what and when. Customer deposits land in the builder personal operating account. The acquisition fee never got formally voted on or disclosed. Distributions stop in month three while carrying costs keep compounding.

We lived a version of that in Florida years ago. Five limited partners put $100k each into a beach property. $500k total. The manager took an undisclosed commission, broke the debt cap, went dark on statements, routed customer cash around the partnership account, and stacked tax allocations to himself. Pay stopped. Trust broke in 90 days.

Luxury spec in Park City has the exact same fault lines, only the kitchen appliances cost more.

A single Promontory build can carry 40 draws, 18 subcontractors, two lenders, and three equity partners across two states. If approvals live in text threads and money lives in three accounts, you cannot prove what happened. Your CPA cannot close the books. Your investor cannot see risk. Your lawyer cannot defend you when a dispute lands.

### Point tools collect data. Systems get work approved and logged.

Substack figured this out for publishing. Real estate is next.

Point solutions are everywhere. You have one app for billing, one for project management, one for document signing, and three different group chats for partner approvals. None of them talk to each other. None of them create a unified audit log.

When an investor asks why a steel change order jumped by $45,000, you should not have to scroll back through three weeks of text messages to find a thumbs-up emoji.

An active joint venture means every partner works and every dollar shows its receipt.

Real duties in writing. Real votes before related party pay. Debt, overruns, tax allocations, and distributions need approval before money moves. Customer funds route to the partnership account only. Every action lands in an audit log with date, amount, and receipt. Documents live versioned in one vault so dark books become impossible.

### The five checks before your next wire

If you are looking at a spec deal this quarter, run this five-point check before you sign anything:

1. Company created with verified legal entity and roles in writing.
2. Operating bank account linked and dedicated exclusively to the entity.
3. JV Terms and operating agreement signed by all capital partners.
4. Capital contributions logged with verified bank receipts.
5. Approval workflows active for any debt, draw, or related party fee.

If your current deal setup cannot clear those five checks in under two minutes on one screen, you are carrying hidden compliance and relationship risk.
