- **GOLD ALLY**: Veteran operator in his late 50s with a bright vibrant look. Gold #FFBD59 on one small lapel pin. Carries a certified wire transfer ledger receipt at standard size.

## Three Act Beats

- **Act I: The Ghosted Wire (Hook)**: Jared stands in a dark glass-walled penthouse overlooking a misty skyline at dawn. A glowing computer monitor shows a failed capital call wire transfer. The Solicitor stands in the shadows across the room, smiling with an unsigned napkin agreement.
- **Act II: The Ledger Proof (Ally)**: Gold Ally enters from the side, stepping into a 45-degree Rembrandt key light. He hands Jared a pristine printed wire transfer ledger showing verified funds and verified corporate entities.
- **Act III: The Takedown (Solution)**: Jared slams the audited entity binder onto the glass table. The Solicitor recoils into the dark corner as the JV OS five-check digital seals flash in electric purple and gold.
