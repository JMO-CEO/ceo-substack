- SEC Rule 506(c) allows general solicitation only if all purchasers are accredited and the issuer takes reasonable steps to verify status. Source: https://www.sec.gov/education/smallbusiness/exemptofferings/rule506c
- FinCEN Residential Real Estate Reporting Rule requires a reporting person to file an online real estate report with 111 fields by month end after closing for non financed entity transfers. Enforcement paused while appeal pending. Maryland AG January 2026 settlement with JV title companies found unlawful fees to agents and brokers. Source: https://www.saul.com/sites/default/files/documents/2026-08/n2026_Real_Estate_Compliance_Outlook.pdf
