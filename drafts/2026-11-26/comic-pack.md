# Comic Pack: The Handshake Trap (W08 Rotation)

## Cast Sheet
- **Hero (Jared Moss):** Man near 50, short gray flecked hair combed back, trimmed salt and pepper goatee, no hat. Back to camera or face in shadow. Coat lining features Electric Purple (#7D12FF) edge glow.
- **Ally (Rich Giles Archetype):** Veteran operator in late 50s, bright vibrant look, graying hair, gold accent on one small object or lapel (#FFBD59), ledgers blank.
- **Villain (The Spreadsheet Broker):** Faceless background figure in deep shadow, wearing dark corporate suit with Toxic Green accent (#00E676), surrounded by messy paperwork and unverified handshake contracts.

## Three Act Beats
- **Act I: The Illusion of Goodwill.** An operator negotiates a multi-million dollar real estate joint venture over a handshake and an unverified spreadsheet, unaware of the structural trap ahead.
- **Act II: The Institutional Standard.** Jared and his ally review audited ledgers and secure accredited verification files, contrasting amateur informality with Blackstone-scale rigor.
- **Act III: Mastery and Acquisition.** Armed with bulletproof operating systems and transparent governance, the syndicate stands sovereign as volatile market conditions clear out unorganized competitors.
