# Outreach prompt, collaboration DM drafts

Goal: start real relationships with rising real estate voices on Substack. Output is drafts only. Sending is manual, by hand, never automated. Substack has no DM API and automation risks the account.

Steps, max 2 searches plus fetches:
1. Check https://substack.com/leaderboard/bestseller/rising for real estate focused names. If the page will not render, websearch for this week's rising real estate bestsellers instead.
2. Skim one recent post each from 3 to 5 names that fit active JV operators.
3. Write drafts/YYYY-MM-DD/dm-pack.md with one DM draft each: one line on their specific post, one line on your angle, one collaboration ask (future article or post together). Short, human, no flattery, no em dashes.
