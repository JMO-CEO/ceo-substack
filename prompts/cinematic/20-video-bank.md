ONE move per video. 10 seconds max, single scene. Image fixes look, prompt directs motion only.
Append LOCK last. No second move, no cut, no morph.

## How to use
1. Start from approved still.
2. Pick ONE V pick.
3. Add speed plus duration plus purpose: what the move reveals.
4. Keep camera motion separate from subject motion. Camera tracks, subject walks.
5. If model drifts, drop to static and generate next beat as separate clip.

## The 18

V01 slow-zoom-in, tension
From a distant fixed window, slowly zoom into a solitary woman waiting at the center of a city square. Eight seconds, no camera translation, no foreground parallax, patient observational mood. She never acknowledges the camera.

V02 push-in, tension
Very slow push in toward [Subject] over 6 seconds, medium into medium close-up.

V03 pull-out, reveal
Slow pull out from [Subject], the frame widening to reveal the environment.

V04 tracking, follow
Side-on continuous shot of a tired security guard trying to pass three obstructing figures in a narrow service corridor. Choreographed non-graphic action, visible pauses and fatigue, full bodies readable, camera tracks only laterally. No rapid edits, no impossible acrobatics, no concealed geography.

V05 static, stillness
In a dark archive, a librarian listens for someone outside. Remove music entirely and retain only quiet ventilation, cloth movement and her restrained breathing. Let the ventilation stop, leaving a single distant latch click clearly exposed. No sudden monster reveal or oversized bass hit.

V06 handheld, realism
Inside a moving minibus, a compact camera pivots from two chatting passengers to the windscreen as a fallen tree blocks the road, then to the driver's reaction and the rear window. One continuous-looking viewpoint, consistent seats, believable window parallax, restrained panic. Build separate interior and exterior plates when necessary.

V07 pov, subjective
At a crowded celebration, a character becomes overwhelmed. Keep the image continuous while voices lose clarity, room detail recedes and a small nearby glass movement becomes unusually prominent. Return gradually to the room when a friend touches her shoulder. Build the perceptual transition in sound post-production.

V08 establishing-static, place
After a daughter leaves the house, hold a static shot of the empty entryway: her shoes are gone, afternoon light remains on the floor and distant street noise continues. No person enters, no camera move, no sentimental insert music. Cut to the next scene only after the room has registered as its own space.

V09 dutch-static, tilt
A tenant confronts a landlord in a narrow stairwell. Static camera canted fifteen degrees, strong diagonal banister, rigid walls and natural anatomy. Both actors remain grounded. No rolling camera, fisheye distortion or vibrating image; the tension comes from the tilted composition.

V10 high-angle lockoff, scale
High oblique view of a lone cleaner crossing a vast empty railway hall after midnight. The architecture and floor pattern dominate. The cleaner pauses at a distant sound. Fixed elevated viewpoint, no drone swoop or zoom.

V11 overhead lockoff, system
Strict top-down locked shot of four people setting a long table, their hands passing plates in a coordinated pattern. The tabletop forms a clean rectangle, no perspective tilt, no camera rotation. One person stops and the system loses its rhythm.

V12 extreme-close-up breath, proof
Extreme close-up of an older watchmaker's eyes as she decides whether to open a sealed mechanism. Frame from brow to upper cheek, natural skin texture, tiny shift in gaze and controlled breathing. Restrained light, stable focus, no cosmetic smoothing or wide-angle facial distortion.

V13 anamorphic night, hero
Night exterior of a woman beneath station lights, widescreen anamorphic-style optics, subtle oval defocused highlights and restrained horizontal flare from one practical light. Natural face width, coherent architecture, no excessive streaks and no letterbox bars baked into the image.

V14 bleach-bypass observe, street
Rain-soaked industrial street with restrained color, dense but separated blacks, pale stressed skin and hard wet highlights. Bleach-bypass-inspired tonal treatment, visible fine texture, no crushed black voids or monochrome conversion. Slow observational camera.

V15 backlight shaft, entry
A conservator enters a dark archive where one high window sends a narrow hard shaft through suspended dust. Her body briefly interrupts the beam, producing a coherent moving shadow in the haze. Deep room, restrained atmosphere, no magical glowing particles.

V16 neon-practical, night city
Neon practicals coloring [Subject], magenta and cyan signs as real keys, wet bounce, mixed gels, night-city chromatics.

V17 rack-focus, ledger reveal
Locked composition: a brass key in sharp foreground on a desk, a woman blurred in the doorway behind it. Hold the key, then slowly rack focus to her expression as the key becomes soft. No camera move, no zoom, neither object changes position.

V18 rain-fog, weather
Use V17 or V02 framing, add: Heavy cinematic rain around [Subject], backlit drops, wet-down pavement, silver streaks. Or: Dense fog around [Subject], depth collapsing, lamps becoming globes, figures appearing late.
